§1

The story

We are an innovation lab. Our job is to bridge cutting-edge research and practical application — to take an idea that is still a paper, a prototype or a hunch, and turn it into software that people can use every day.

In practice that means one team covering the whole path: websites and web apps, mobile applications, enterprise SaaS, and AI and LLM systems. Design, engineering and research sit at the same table, so what we prototype is what we ship.

We work from two hubs. Pennsylvania, USA is our operations hub; Vodra, Rajshahi, Bangladesh is our engineering and R&D hub. Our flagship product is Mini Me.

How we hold ourselves

Four principles, and what each one means in practice.

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
Evidence over opinionWe prototype first and put it in front of real users, so a decision rests on what we observed rather than on what we assumed.
Built to be handed overCode is tested and documented, with monitoring in place, so any competent engineer can pick it up — including you.
One team, end to endDesign, engineering and AI research are done by the same people, so nothing is lost between a brief and a release.
